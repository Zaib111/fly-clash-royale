    A fruit fly's brain, playing Clash Royale

    The player called Fly is controlled by a simulation of the adult fruit-fly brain wired exactly like the real one: 134,181 neurons and 2.7 million connections from the FlyWire connectome. Which neurons connect, and whether each connection excites or inhibits, comes from the real fly and never changes.

    Every half-second of game time, the arena is projected onto the fly's ~7,500 photoreceptors. Activity spreads through the wiring, and the descending & motor neurons (pink in the brain view) are read out as its choice: wait, or play a card somewhere.

    It learned only by playing against copies of itself (reinforcement learning, PPO): wins are rewarded and losses penalised. Training adjusted how strongly each neuron responds, never the wiring. The bots never taught it; they only measure it.

    Controls: click a card (or 1–4), then the arena. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, B to expand the brain, Space to pause, R for a rematch.
